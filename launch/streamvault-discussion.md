Hi! I've built **Open TV Atlas**, a free playlist picker that may be useful to people setting up M3U sources in StreamVault.

It lets you choose a **language + category + quality** and copy one playlist URL. For example, English sports in HD or Farsi movies. English appears first, Farsi second, followed by the wider language directory.

- **Try the picker:** https://iman-py.github.io/open-tv-atlas/
- **Browser walkthrough and setup:** https://iman-py.github.io/open-tv-atlas/#demo
- **Source and playlists:** https://github.com/IMAN-Py/open-tv-atlas

The stream links and channel metadata come from **iptv-org**, whose community deserves the credit for maintaining that data. Atlas adds filtering, quality variants, and a simpler way to choose and share playlist URLs. The repository rebuilds them daily.

I included StreamVault as the suggested Android TV player, linking to your official releases. The demo shows the browser picker only; it is not a claim that I've playback-tested every stream or completed device-side StreamVault testing. This is an independent project, not an official integration.

If you try an Atlas playlist in StreamVault, I'd appreciate feedback on import behavior and category grouping. Please include the player version and playlist URL when reporting a problem. Availability can depend on the broadcaster and your region.

Thanks for building StreamVault, and for the work of the iptv-org community.
