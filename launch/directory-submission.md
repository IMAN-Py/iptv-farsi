### Description

Open TV Atlas is a free IPTV playlist directory and generator built on iptv-org data. Users can search for a language, combine it with a category and HD or SD quality, and copy a stable M3U URL for their player. The repository refreshes playlists daily, and the browser picker loads its latest index. English and Farsi are easy to reach, with a wider worldwide language directory, setup instructions, and a browser walkthrough. It does not host video, provide paid subscriptions, or claim every stream is working.
